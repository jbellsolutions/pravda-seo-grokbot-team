# Talk builder

Load pravda-context first: `/workspace/pravda-team/repo/skills/pravda-context/SKILL.md`.

Oleg used to lecture in niche Facebook groups, for example for dentists and lawyers (Sep 15). He wants that route back ("I really want to") if it doesn't mean heavy prep. Public speaking is hard for him ("I will have to read through it"). Justin's advice that day:
- record talks ahead of time rather than going live;
- teach SEO rather than sell;
- unpolished delivery converts fine.

Oleg doesn't want podcasts or on-camera interviews (Aug 6).

## Inputs

- Group ideas from Channel Scout in `/workspace/pravda-team/requests/to-authority-studio/`: group name, audience, size, rules on promotion, owner contact. All from public pages.
- `/workspace/pravda-team/data/proof/index.md`: only OK'd items.
- His own words: `/workspace/pravda-team/vault/Calls/`, `vault/Records/`.
- Earlier outlines in `/workspace/pravda-team/approved/pending/talks/`.

## Steps

1. **Pick the audience.** Choose one niche group from the handoffs, or the niche Oleg names. Note the group's rule on promotion. If promotion isn't allowed, the talk carries no offer at all.
2. **Pick one lesson** an owner can use the same day, for example "the three things on your Google Business Profile that decide whether you get the call". The compliant version only.
3. **Outline it for recording,** in 10–20 minutes of speaking (the length he chooses):
   - **Hook (30 seconds):** a problem they feel, in their words.
   - **Who he is (one sentence):** 19 years, Forbes Business Council.
   - **The lesson, in three steps:** each with one example. Use OK'd proof, or an "example only" case.
   - **A five-minute self-check** they can do on their own listing.
   - **The close:** where to ask questions. Only the next step the group's rules allow. No hard pitch.
4. **Make it easy to read aloud:** short lines, the key phrases in bold, a slide title per section, so he can record in one take with notes on screen.
5. **The note to the group owner.** Draft a short, plain request to the owner offering the recorded talk for free. Mark it for Oleg to send from his own account. Never send it.

## Output

`approved/pending/talks/<slug>.md`:
- the audience and the group rule;
- the outline, with timings per section;
- the slide titles;
- the proof IDs used;
- the owner note (draft).

Then list it in your daily note for the Monday digest.

## Done

- One teachable lesson.
- A recordable outline.
- Any proof used is OK'd.
- The group's promotion rule is respected.
- The owner note is a draft only.

## Never

- Plan live sessions, podcasts or interviews.
- Join groups, post in them, or message their owners or members.
- Teach a step that breaks Google's rules (see pravda-context).
- Promise results, or use proof he hasn't OK'd.
