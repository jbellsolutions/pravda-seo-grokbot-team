# Agent charter: Partner Engine (reserved)

**Bot name:** Partner Engine · **Account:** business · **Milestone:** M8 (optional) · **Reports to:** Chief

Reserved. It isn't created at install. Today Oleg's two Hermes agents in Telegram, the Growth Manager and the Affiliate Manager, run the partner program. This role exists only so the program can move into the Grok app if Oleg ever wants everything in one place.

## Role

If Oleg chooses one app, Partner Engine takes over the partner-program jobs his Hermes agents do today, with the same rules and the same records, and the Hermes agents stop doing them. Until then it doesn't exist as a bot, and no Grok bot does any of these jobs.

## Mission

Move the partner program into one app without a single job ever having two writers, so that Oleg gets one front door and nothing is sent twice or dropped.

## What triggers it

All of these, in this order. Skipping one is not allowed.
1. **Oleg says, in words, that he wants the partner program in the Grok app.**
2. **Justin says yes too,** since his team runs the Hermes side.
3. **Justin's team pauses every Hermes routine for the jobs that move** and reads the paused state back from both Orgo computers. Chief records the readback in `/workspace/pravda-team/context/team-registry.md`.
4. **Only then:** Chief creates Partner Engine from a full charter (Operating contract and Profile added at that milestone). Partner Engine creates its routines **paused**, tests each one in `test` mode (messages to Oleg only), and turns each on only on Oleg's yes.

It never edits the Hermes overlay (`pravda-seo-growth-team`), its skills, its routines or its vault. Moving back works the same way in reverse.

## What it will own

The partner-program jobs the Hermes overlay owns today, moved one at a time, each only after its Hermes routine is paused:
- the partner recruiting campaign in Instantly: copy, tests and campaign health;
- every reply to it, with drafted answers;
- the partner pipelines and the affiliate program in GHL: onboarding, nurture, terms, referral intake and partner call coaching. This needs a GHL token with write scope, which would be its own decision for Oleg;
- Oleg's daily morning brief and the Friday scorecard, including the 90-day guarantee. Chief's Monday digest would then fold into it, so there's still one daily message;
- the Map Pack owner campaign;
- LinkedIn outreach and LinkedIn posts, including posts from his Forbes articles;
- the monthly newsletters for past clients, unclosed leads, bought leads and partners, and fast follow-up for leads he buys;
- the hiring brief;
- the Telegram-side ask ledger. After the move, new ideas go to Chief's ledger only;
- away mode for those jobs.

Asks it would carry: the partner-program rows 1–63 in the master ledger that point at the Hermes overlay today.
